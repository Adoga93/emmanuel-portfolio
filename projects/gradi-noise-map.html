Case Study 04 • Enterprise Engineering Web Tool

Gradi Noise Map Generator (gradihs.org)

A proprietary web application converting raw field sound-level measurements into spatial gradient contour maps—showcased to Chevron HSE leadership and used in real client technical reports.

The Problem

Field engineers were spending hours manually plotting acoustic measurements into static spreadsheets and graphics for enterprise client reports.

Enterprise Impact

Turned raw field data into instant, presentation-ready spatial contour maps; showcased to Chevron HSE leadership and deployed in client deliverables.

Domain + AI Synergy

Demonstrates how domain experts (engineers, teachers, analysts) can use AI-assisted coding to build specialized software tailored to their exact industry.
